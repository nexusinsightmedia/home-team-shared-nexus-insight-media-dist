Best VPN Services in 2025: We Tested 12 VPNs for Speed, Privacy, and Streaming

By Nexus Insight Media | Last Updated: May 2025

Do You Actually Need a VPN?

If you work remotely, use public Wi-Fi, travel frequently, or just value your online privacy, a VPN is essential. But not all VPNs are created equal — some are blazing fast but log your data, others are private but too slow for streaming. We tested 12 leading VPNs across speed, privacy, streaming capability, and ease of use.

Best Overall: NordVPN

Rating: 9.5/10 Best for: Most users

Pricing

  • Monthly: $12.99/month
  • 1-Year: $4.99/month (save 62%)
  • 2-Year: $3.69/month (save 72%)

What We Love

  • Blazing fast speeds (our tests showed only 11% speed loss)
  • Massive server network — 6,300+ servers in 111 countries
  • Double VPN and Onion Over VPN for maximum privacy
  • Threat Protection blocks ads, trackers, and malware
  • Meshnet allows secure file sharing between devices
  • Works flawlessly with Netflix, Hulu, BBC iPlayer, and Disney+
  • Strict no-logs policy (audited multiple times)

What Could Be Better

  • Desktop app can feel a bit cluttered
  • No free tier (30-day money-back guarantee instead)
  • Some advanced features are buried in settings

Verdict

NordVPN is the complete package. It's fast enough for gaming and streaming, secure enough for journalists and privacy advocates, and easy enough for beginners. The 2-year plan is the best value in VPNs.

Best for Privacy: Mullvad VPN

Rating: 9.0/10 Best for: Privacy purists

Pricing

  • Flat Rate: €5/month (~$5.50) — no discounts, no tiers
  • Payment: Cash, Monero, Bitcoin, credit card, PayPal

What We Love

  • The most privacy-focused VPN on the market
  • Anonymous account — no email, no personal info required
  • Accepts cash payments by mail (yes, actual cash)
  • Open-source apps with public audits
  • WireGuard and OpenVPN support
  • RAM-only servers (no data written to disk)

What Could Be Better

  • Smaller server network (800+ servers in 43 countries)
  • Slower speeds than NordVPN (about 25% speed loss)
  • No streaming optimization (may not work with all services)
  • Basic interface (no fancy features — that's by design)

Verdict

If privacy is your #1 priority, Mullvad is unbeatable. The flat €5/month pricing is refreshingly honest, and the anonymity-first approach is unmatched. Just don't expect streaming optimizations or a flashy app.

Best for Streaming: ExpressVPN

Rating: 9.0/10 Best for: Accessing geo-restricted content

Pricing

  • Monthly: $12.95/month
  • 6-Month: $9.99/month
  • 1-Year: $8.32/month (includes 3 months free)

What We Love

  • Consistently unblocks Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu, BBC iPlayer, and more
  • Lightway protocol is incredibly fast and reliable
  • TrustedServer technology (RAM-only, no data written to disk)
  • Works in restrictive countries (China, UAE, Turkey)
  • Excellent 24/7 customer support

What Could Be Better

  • Expensive compared to competitors
  • Only 5 simultaneous connections (Mullvad allows 5, NordVPN allows 6)
  • Parent company is based in the British Virgin Islands (privacy-friendly, but ownership could be more transparent)

Verdict

ExpressVPN is the streaming champion. If your primary use case is watching content from other countries, this is the VPN to get. The speed and reliability are top-notch, though you pay a premium.

Best Budget: Surfshark

Rating: 8.5/10 Best for: Families and users on a budget

Pricing

  • Monthly: $15.45/month
  • 1-Year: $3.99/month
  • 2-Year: $2.49/month

What We Love

  • Unlimited simultaneous devices (one account covers your whole family)
  • CleanWeb blocks ads and malware
  • NoBorders mode works in restrictive countries
  • GPS override on mobile (hides your real location)
  • Good speeds (about 18% speed loss)
  • 30-day money-back guarantee

What Could Be Better

  • Smaller server network (3,200+ in 100 countries)
  • Some servers can be inconsistent
  • Customer support isn't always helpful

Verdict

For $2.49/month with unlimited devices, Surfshark is incredible value. It's perfect for families or anyone with many devices. It does everything well enough without excelling at any one thing.

Which VPN Should You Choose?

  1. NordVPN — The best all-around VPN. Fast, private, and works everywhere. The 2-year plan at $3.69/month is a steal.
  2. Mullvad — If absolute privacy is non-negotiable. Pay with cash, create an anonymous account, sleep easy.
  3. ExpressVPN — If you primarily need to unblock streaming services. The most reliable for Netflix and BBC iPlayer.
  4. Surfshark — If you have many devices or a tight budget. Unlimited simultaneous connections at $2.49/month.

What to Look for in a VPN

  • No-logs policy — Look for independently audited no-logs claims
  • Kill switch — Cuts internet if VPN drops (prevents IP leaks)
  • RAM-only servers — Data is wiped on reboot (better privacy)
  • WireGuard protocol — Newer, faster, more secure than OpenVPN
  • Independent audits — Trust but verify
